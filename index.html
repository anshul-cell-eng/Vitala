02 — VISION

NOT JUST
A NUMBER.

Traditional wearables show isolated numbers: a heart rate spike, an ambient temperature reading, or a step count. In high-risk environments, isolated numbers mislead.

VITALA creates multi-modal context: combining cardiovascular exertion with ambient thermal load, toxic particulate inhalation, and biometric posture to forecast crisis before it occurs.
